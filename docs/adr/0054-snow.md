# ADR-0054 · M24l: snow

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Extends:** [ADR-0029](0029-city-weather-dressing.md) (weather),
  [ADR-0051](0051-time-of-day.md) (time of day and weathers),
  [ADR-0044](0044-street-and-cab.md) (exit street)

## Context

Since M24j there are three weathers — clear, fog, rain — and together with four times
of day twelve combinations. ADR-0051 (decision 2) promised a fourth weather, snow, and
sixteen combinations. The milestone plan has falling snow, snow on the roof, cornices
and the street, footprints, its own sound and wind, cars and pedestrians in the snow,
the city in a snowfall.

**Check against the original.** The 1983 arcade has no weather at all: black emptiness
around the building in all ROM palettes, no port knows weather (ADR-0051). Snow, like
rain, is an extension of the remake; the closest period reference is the winter
section of Spy Hunter (1983). The arcade has no pedestrians either: it has no exit
street, the car leaves past the edge of the screen.

## Decisions

Questions asked of the user on 2026-10-02.

1. **Four weathers, 25 % each.** Snow is the fourth [`Weather.Kind`], the draw is even.
   Weather for all seeds is reshuffled; tests tied to a seed's weather take it from
   [`Weather.of_seed`], not from memory.
2. **Snow lies from the start, with footprints.** The cover already lies on the roof,
   cornices, equipment and the exit street from the first frame of the building and
   does not grow. On the roof and at the exit footprints of Otto and agents remain, on
   the road — car tracks.
3. **Pedestrians — in any weather.** At the exit pedestrians from the Quaternius packs
   walk along the sidewalk; in snow — in coats, hats and scarves, in rain — in raincoats
   and under umbrellas, at night there are fewer of them. A new
   part of the street, not only for snow.
4. **A snowy roof is slippery.** On the roof in snow Otto and agents brake with
   sliding. A divergence from the arcade — mechanics depend on weather for the first
   time; combat is checked by a bot run on snowy seeds.
5. Its own sound — wind and the crunch of footsteps on snow; snow falls drifting with
   the wind, the city behind the building is in a snowfall. There are no thunderstorms
   in snow.

### Additions during the milestone

The user's requests during the milestone (2026-10-02): "snow must be flawless, like
the rain", "react to the wind from the helicopter blades", "natural-looking, not just
round white dots", "recheck the rain physics", "animate the pedestrians… make them
different", "dress the pedestrians for the weather".

6. **A flake is a clump, not a dot.** Its own shader (`snow_flake.gdshader`): five soft
   blobs, each flake with its own pattern, rotation and sway in the frame plane, lit by
   lamps and the sun — brighter against the light. Snow has no particle turbulence: in
   Godot it is mixed into the velocity every step, and even half a percent per step kept
   the flakes bunched up near the sky.
7. **Precipitation dies on everything solid.** The roof — by a height map (`RoofCatch`,
   shared by rain and snow), the exit street — by its own map from the street layer:
   drops and flakes no longer go through awnings and cars to the asphalt on a timer.
   People, cars and the helicopter — by particle catchers on the body (`Shelter`): before
   the milestone rain went through Otto and agents. The helicopter's catcher follows the
   fuselage, without the rotor disc and the rope.
8. **The rotor downwash** blows both rain and snow (`Downwash.gust`), and in snow it
   raises snow dust from the cover. The dust is soft puffs in a fan along the roof:
   before the milestone it was drawn as squares and flew out in a ring in front of the
   facade.
9. **Snow also lies on the traffic cars** — as an overlay material that moves with the
   body (`snow_cap.gdshader`) — on the car at the curb, on the city roofs.
10. **Sound** chosen by the user by ear from the listening page: a blizzard outside —
    kyles 454213 (CC0), footsteps on snow — Lumamorph 615658 (CC0), tires on slush —
    Zabuhailo 190997 (CC-BY 4.0).
11. **Pedestrians** — Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men and Women packs (CC0,
    `tools/build_people.py`): each is assembled from the head, torso, legs and shoes of
    different models of their sex, with their own hair, skin and height, clothing from a
    palette of city colors, one item — one color. The pack's walk is looped and matched
    to the stride — from glTF it came without a loop, and the pedestrian floated like a
    statue. Pedestrians have none of the pack's weapons.

    **Clothing by weather and time of day** — a revision across all sixteen
    combinations (the user's request: "there must be no bare body parts"):

    | Weather and time | How dressed |
    |---|---|
    | clear, morning and day | light: T-shirts, dresses, shorts |
    | clear, evening and night; fog | a long-sleeved sweater or jacket in the top's color, gloves, trousers, closed shoes |
    | rain | a raincoat for most, the rest — as for cool weather; an umbrella in hand |
    | snow | a coat for everyone, gloves, trousers, boots; many have a knitted cuffed hat and a scarf; no umbrellas |

    Except for the light outfit, only the face is uncovered: the skin of the arms, legs
    and feet is clothed, legs are taken only from models in trousers. Sleeves avoid skin
    colors: a beige sleeve read as a bare arm. Coat skirts are not made as a part on a
    bone — a rigid hem on the hips read as a bag.

    **The umbrella is carried in hand**
    ([`UmbrellaGrip`](../../src/levels/umbrella_grip.gd); the user's remarks — "a person
    should carry the umbrella", "the hand does not hold the umbrella"): on top of the
    walk the hand nearer the camera holds the shaft at the chest — elbow bent, hand
    with fingers up, fingers clenched into a fist around the shaft; the shaft passes
    vertically through the fist, the umbrella sways with the step. The canopy is convex.
    In snow nobody walks under umbrellas.

### Slippery roof measurement

`playthrough.gd --agents --endless --weather=3` versus `--weather=0`, seeds 1–6, skill
0: 14 deaths in snow, 11 in clear weather. On five seeds of six the run matched step for
step — the bot spends seconds on the roof — on the second, snow cost three deaths. The
bot passes the building on any seed; the 9 m/s² grip is kept.

## Consequences

- Sixteen combinations of time and weather; tests that go through all combinations go
  through snow too.
- Sliding is the first mechanics rule that depends on weather; its numbers are tuned by
  bot measurement, like combat.
