# ADR-0015 · Round palette and roof after the Spectrum version

- **Status:** accepted; items 1–2 changed — the port's palette
  ([ADR-0017](0017-spectrum-palette-and-shafts.md), decision 1), then the round palette via
  materials and light ([ADR-0019](0019-3d-pivot.md), decision 7;
  [ADR-0029](0029-city-weather-dressing.md), decision 5); item 5 — threshold silhouette,
  [ADR-0024](0024-building-geometry.md), decision 2
- **Date:** 2026-09-16

## Context

A question from outside: will the buildings be all alike — colours and structure do not change?

Checking the code confirmed: yes. From building to building (`src/main.gd:132`) exactly three
things change — the layout by seed, the city silhouette behind the windows and `agent_menace`.
Everything else is the same, and the colours are hard-coded as constants in `greybox_level.gd`:

```gdscript
const AMBIENT := Color(0.50, 0.54, 0.68)      # overall building tone
const FLOOR_LIGHT := Color(1.0, 0.95, 0.86)   # fill of a lit floor
const SHAFT_LIGHT := Color(0.78, 0.86, 1.0)   # light column in the shaft
const CITY := Color(0.12, 0.14, 0.24)
```

Environment textures are generated by `tools/render_env.py` from the palette in
`tools/palette.py` — one set for the whole game.

### What the arcade says

> The basic building layout never changes, though colors change with new levels. Each
> building's layout remains more or less the same, but the red doors are always in
> different areas and the enemy agents are craftier.

That is, the original is arranged the opposite way to ours: the floor plan is roughly one, and
the palette is new each time. This is a hole in the check before M5 —
[ADR-0008](0008-building-generation.md), item 2 quoted the same thought about "levels
essentially the same", but missed the colour change.

### What the Spectrum version shows

It is chosen as the reference. The port is genuine: 1987, Quicksilva (Bug-Byte re-release),
developed by Binary Design, music by David Whittaker, 48K/128K with AY. Press: Crash 72%,
Sinclair User 4/5, Your Sinclair 8/10.

In-game shots from archives were analysed. What they show:

- **Colour language:** a black void background, turquoise floor interiors, yellow doors, red
  brickwork on the sides, white slabs with a black dashed line, blue sprites, a yellow HUD
  (`SCORE / HI SCORE / LIVES / ROUND`). Flat attribute colours, no lighting at all.
- **Roof:** at the top stands a small machine-room superstructure above the shaft — its own
  gable roof, brick sides, a cab inside. Otto descends to it **by rope**: a yellow dashed line
  goes up off the screen.
- **Floors run the full width:** the brickwork reaches both edges of the screen.
- The level counter is called `ROUND`.

**A colour change from round to round in the Spectrum version could not be confirmed:** both
available in-game shots are `ROUND 01`, and the review texts are not openly available. The
decision below relies on a direct quote about the arcade, not on the port.

### A correction about the silhouette

The stepped silhouette from [ADR-0014](0014-building-architecture.md), item 3 relied on secondary
reviews of the arcade. **The Spectrum version's shots do not confirm it** — there the floors are
of one width, and at the top there is just a superstructure. This is recorded here so that the
decision does not look more confirmed than it is.

## Decisions

### 1. The reference is level structure, not look

From the Spectrum version we take rules: a palette per round, a superstructure with a rope on
the roof, `ROUND` as the counter. It is drawn by our own engine with the lighting from M6.

Flat attribute colours and the black background **are not taken**. They would supersede
[ADR-0002](0002-visual-target.md) and [ADR-0011](0011-asset-pipeline.md) and devalue M6 and M7
entirely: with a flat fill neither normals, nor highlights, nor the lighting system are needed.
The cost does not match the gain.

### 2. The palette lives in the building rules and changes by round

Colours move from level constants into a palette that `BuildingRules.for_building()` picks by
building number. The set of palettes is finite and cycles — as in the original, where rounds go
around in a loop.

Tinting can be done on top of the finished assets without regenerating them: the diffuse is
multiplied by the palette colour, the normal and highlight stay the same, and light keeps falling
on the relief.

### 3. A darkened floor stays readable — and a test checks it

The main trap: `AMBIENT` is not only the building tone but also **the tone of a darkened floor**
([ADR-0010](0010-lighting-and-atmosphere.md), item 3). It is cold on purpose: a darkened floor
differs from a lit one by hue, not brightness, because in the dark agents keep shooting and
"just darker" would mean death for nothing.

So the palette sets **both** pairs — the tone of a lit floor and the tone of a darkened one — and
a test makes sure enough of a gap remains between them in every palette of the set. A freely
wandering building colour will sooner or later produce a warm building where a darkened floor
does not read, and it will be the player who finds it.

### 4. A superstructure appears on the roof, and Otto descends onto it by rope

The top shaft already reaches the roof (ADR-0014, item 2) — the superstructure stands over its
opening. The rope is an intro, not a mechanic: Otto slides down it at the start of the building
and from then on plays as usual.

### 5. The stepped silhouette stays

Despite the correction above. It gives a readable difficulty gradient — a narrow simple top, a
wide tangled bottom — and this is confirmed by arcade reviews directly. The superstructure with
the rope sits on top of it and does not get in the way of anything.

## What is not in the milestone

- **The flat Spectrum look** — decision 1.
- **Interior walls dividing the floor in two**, and shafts of different lengths. Both bring their
  own reachability-graph pass — ADR-0014, "What is not in the milestone".
- **Initially dark floors.** Currently a floor goes dark only from a shot-down lamp.
- **Changing the palette within one building.** The palette is picked per round and does not
  change.
