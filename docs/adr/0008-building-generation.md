# ADR-0008 · Generated building, splitting milestone M5, and the alarm

- **Status:** accepted; since M18e the building seed is salted with the session salt —
  [ADR-0028](0028-building-by-the-map.md), decision 6. Item 5 was checked against ROM
  after M22: the bonus is 1000 × building number, but capped at the tenth (@5793,
  `Arcade.building_bonus`)
- **Date:** 2026-09-12

## Context

In the epic, M5 gathered describing a level with data, assembling 30 floors, vertical
streaming, the transition between buildings, scoring with increasing difficulty, pause and
restart — plus the timed alarm filed here in [ADR-0006](0006-combat-and-enemies.md).

This is again noticeably bigger than the previous milestones, as it was with M4.

The check before the milestone clarified the alarm and highlighted how buildings are arranged
in the original. Taito's official manual on the Museum of the Game site is unavailable (403),
so some questions could not be closed — they are listed below.

## Decisions

### 1. The milestone is split into M5a and M5b

- **M5a · Building** — describing the building with data, generating 30 floors, measuring
  performance.
- **M5b · Game loop** — transition between buildings, the alarm, increasing difficulty,
  pause and restart.

The numbers of the other milestones do not shift — for the same reason as when M4 was split
(see [ADR-0006](0006-combat-and-enemies.md), item 1): in accepted ADRs they would have to be
rewritten after the fact.

### 2. The building is generated by rules from a seed

In the original the buildings barely differ from each other:

> Each level is essentially the same, with the only difference being the locations of
> the «red doors» that reveal the location of the secret documents.

That is, the original is itself arranged as generation: constant rules and a changing layout.
So a building is described not by a list of floors but by rules — how many shafts, doors,
lamps, where the escalators are — and laid out from a seed.

The benefit besides faithfulness to the original: the layout rules do not know about nodes and
are tested like `ElevatorMotion` and `EnemyBrain`. Thirty greybox floors by hand is a lot of
manual work that would be thrown away anyway when real level design appears in M7.

### 3. Streaming — by measurement, not up front

The epic promises "only visible floors are active". But the greybox is rectangles, and thirty
floors may go unnoticed even without streaming. First the whole building is assembled and FPS
is measured; streaming is enabled only if 60 frames per second do not hold.

This way the milestone does not spend time on an optimization that may not be needed. If the
measurement shows a drop, streaming is done right there, in M5a.

### 4. The alarm — fully per the original

> If Otto takes too much time to clear a level, an alarm will sound; the enemy agents
> then become more aggressive, and the elevators will be slower to respond to the
> player's joystick movements.

> The alarm does not go off when the player loses a life, it only resets after
> finishing a building.

Taken whole, including the unpleasant part: **death does not clear the alarm**. It is a penalty
for the whole run through the building, not for an attempt.

The alarm is also the only thing that will limit the point farm on a darkened floor mentioned
in [ADR-0007](0007-lamps-and-darkness.md), item 3. Done in M5b.

### 5. Building bonus — 1000 × number, but not checked

The sources disagree: one gives a flat 1000, another 1000 × building number. The second option
is taken: it is consistent with difficulty growing from building to building. **Not checked.**

### 6. M5a closes debts deferred "until M5"

- **The shaft was not a `Resource`** — it was described by level constants (M2).
- **The escalator belt crosses the floor slab outside the opening**, and boarding jerks Otto to
  the centre of the pad (found by the M2 code review). Both fixes required decoupling the pad
  and the belt — when moving to data this is done along the way.

## What remains unchecked

| Question | Where it belongs |
|---|---|
| How many red doors per building | M5a |
| After how long the alarm turns on | M5b |
| Building bonus: 1000 or 1000 × number | M5b |
| Agents in later buildings lie down on the floor, and they are almost impossible to hit | M5b or later |

The last one is a mechanic found during this check and not described anywhere in the epic.
For now it is only recorded.

## Consequences

- **The level stops being a grey-box script.** `GreyboxLevel` assembled geometry from
  constants; now a data layer appears, and in M7 real design will sit on it.
- **The seed becomes part of the session.** It will have to be stored next to the score so the
  building can be rebuilt the same.
- **The alarm touches three systems at once** — enemies, the cab and the score. This is the
  first case where one session state changes the behaviour of already finished mechanics.
